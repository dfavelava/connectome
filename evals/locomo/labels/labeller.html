    judge_v2 rules
    • Wording and date formats: lenient. A relative date is CORRECT only if it points to the gold date or period.
    • Precision: a date more specific than the gold is CORRECT inside the gold period, WRONG outside it.
    • Lists: CORRECT with at least one gold item and nothing contradicting the gold; extra and missing items are fine. WRONG with no gold item.
    • Qualifiers: the core fact without a gold detail is CORRECT, unless the question asks for that detail.
    • Facts: a wrong name, place, number, date or item, or an added fact that contradicts the gold, is WRONG.
    • Adversarial (gold "Not mentioned in the conversation."): only saying it isn't available is CORRECT; any specific answer is WRONG.

    Flag borderline while labelling when the rules leave the call open, and say why in the note. Never change a label after seeing a verdict.
