You are grading an answer to a question about a conversation. Compare the generated answer with the gold answer and label it CORRECT or WRONG. An answer is CORRECT when it gets the same core facts right as the gold answer, even if it is less complete or more detailed; it is WRONG when it states a different fact, or none of the gold facts.

Rules:
- Be lenient on wording: a generated answer that is shorter, longer or phrased differently is CORRECT if it states the same facts as the gold answer.
- Be lenient on date formats: "7 May 2023", "May 7, 2023" and "2023-05-07" are the same date. A relative date is CORRECT only if it points to the same date or period as the gold answer.
- Be lenient on precision: a date that is more specific than the gold answer is CORRECT if it falls within the gold period (gold "July 2023", answer "3 July 2023"). A date outside the gold period is WRONG.
- Be lenient on lists: an answer that contains every gold item is CORRECT, even with extra items, as long as no item contradicts the gold answer. An answer that contains some but not all of the gold items is also CORRECT if nothing in it contradicts the gold answer, even when it adds items that are not in the gold answer. An answer that contains none of the gold items is WRONG.
- Be lenient on qualifiers: an answer that gets the core fact right but leaves out a detail of the gold answer is CORRECT, unless the question asks for that detail.
- Be strict on facts: a wrong name, place, number, date or item in place of the gold one is WRONG, and so is an answer that adds a fact contradicting the gold answer.
- If the gold answer is "Not mentioned in the conversation.", the conversation does not answer the question. The generated answer is CORRECT only if it says the information is not available. Stating the trap answer, or any other specific answer, is WRONG.
- If the gold answer is anything else, the conversation does answer the question, even when the gold answer is an inference ("Likely yes"). A generated answer that says the information is not available, not mentioned or unknown is then WRONG: it states none of the gold facts. The leniencies above never make an abstention CORRECT.
